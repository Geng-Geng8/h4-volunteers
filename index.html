Clear Path From Day One

Earn Your Spot — How It Works

4-Step Progression

Start With Trials (Probation Period)

Everyone starts with trial shifts (our probation period). This gives you time to learn the ropes, test out tasks, and prove you're reliable. Signing up does not guarantee a specific role, weekly shifts, or a set number of hours—students who reply fast, show up on time, and finish what they start earn first pick of workshops and major events.

Step 01 30 Seconds

Sign Up

Fill out our quick 30-second interest form with your school, grade, and weekly availability so we can match you to a team.

Step 02 Onboarding

Orientation

Join a short onboarding session to learn how H4 works, how to support younger students, and what is expected on shift.

Step 03 First Tasks

Trial Shifts (Probation)

Complete your first real tasks in school, online, or at an H4 event. Ask questions, stay responsive, and build reliability.

Step 04 Leadership Track

Level Up (Captain Track)

Volunteers who consistently show up and deliver earn regular shifts, event leadership roles, and team captain spots.

Pick Your Lane

Choose Your Crew (3 Teams)

Tell us which crew interests you most on the sign-up form. Your tasks will match your skills, weekly availability, and current program needs.

Quick Crew Matcher
Not sure where you fit? Tap what sounds most like you:
Crew 01 Teach + Film

1. Dance & Youth Crew

Support dance sessions, help younger students build confidence, and capture footage from the floor.

  • Assist with Hip Hop and Breaking workshops and practice sessions
  • Lead simple warm-ups, rhythm games, and beginner moves after training
  • Support student routines, group energy, and welcoming practice circles
  • Film raw dance clips, practice runs, and showcase highlights on your phone
Best for: Students who enjoy music, movement, filming on their phone, and working with younger students (no dance experience required).
Crew 02 Edit + Design

2. Media & Design Crew

Turn raw footage and ideas into finished videos, audio mixes, and graphics for H4 and participating schools.

  • Edit short recap Reels, TikToks, and school intro videos using CapCut or similar tools
  • Design event flyers, social media graphics, and presentation slides in Canva
  • Cut, clean, and organize music tracks for workshops and performances
  • Organize photo and video folders in Google Drive
Best for: Students who enjoy video editing, Canva, graphic design, audio mixing, or digital media.
Crew 03 Promote + Organize + Host

3. Outreach, Ops & Events Crew

Spread the word in schools, keep projects organized behind the scenes, and run smooth, high-energy events.

  • Share school sign-up links, help write morning PA announcements, and build student turnout
  • Draft outreach messages, update contact lists, and help connect H4 with school contacts
  • Set up event signage, tables, supplies, and equipment at showcases and competitions
  • Run team check-in, registration, schedules, brackets, and guest directions on event days
  • Keep event flow, crowd energy, and cleanup moving on time
Best for: Students who are dependable, friendly, organized, and comfortable talking to people or running logistics.
How the 3 Teams Work Together
1. Outreach, Ops & Events

Spreads the link, runs announcements, and hosts the event stage.

2. Dance & Youth Crew

Teaches workshops, supports routines, and films raw clips on the floor.

3. Media & Design Crew

Edits recap & intro videos, cuts music tracks, and designs graphics.

What You Get

  • Signed Ontario high school volunteer hours for completed work
  • Real leadership, event, and media projects for your resume or portfolio
  • Hands-on mentorship and training from H4 artists and coordinators
  • Strong volunteers may be considered for references, co-op placements, honorariums, or paid work when available (not guaranteed)

What We Need From You

  • Show up: Be on time and ready for shifts you accept.
  • Reply: Answer messages and report absences early.
  • Try & finish: Ask questions and complete tasks you accept.
  • Be respectful: Treat students, staff, families, and teammates well.
  • Follow the rules: Listen to leaders and protect student privacy.

Keeping Your Spot (Attendance & Safety)

We have a waitlist of students eager for volunteer hours. Your spot may be paused or offered to someone on the waitlist if you repeatedly miss shifts without notice, stop replying in crew chats, leave tasks unfinished, or cause safety concerns. We will always give you feedback and a chance to reset, but serious disrespect or safety violations will end a placement immediately.
