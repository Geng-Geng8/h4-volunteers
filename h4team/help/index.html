Get Help

Talk to your H4 lead, a teacher or another trusted adult when you need support.

If something feels unsafe

Stop and talk to a trusted adult, school staff or the H4 director right away. You can go straight to an adult for bullying, harassment, injury, privacy concerns or anything that feels wrong.

In an immediate emergency, call 911 or alert school staff. Keep incident details private and let adults handle the response.

Use approved channels

  • Use a school-approved channel with a named adult moderator. Ask for an alternative if you can’t join.
  • Stay respectful and keep messages about H4. Give people time to respond and avoid pressuring them after hours.
  • Keep private details, screenshots of records, student lists, permission forms and unapproved photos out of public pages and chats.

Know who can say yes

Students can choose tasks, make drafts, ask teachers about interest and help their teams. Adults approve events, public posts, media, spending and partnerships. They handle consent, sensitive records, complaints and official hours.

When a task is stuck

Tell your lead which part is hard and what you’ve tried. Ask for one next step, another task or a break. School, health and family come first.
