Lead Your Team

A playbook for setting up your crew, assigning tasks and checking in.

One goal. One task each.

Your job is to make the next step clear. Give students a choice, help when they get stuck, and celebrate work that helps the team.

Choose a school mission
Set up your team

Confirm your school and teacher contact with the director. Set up an approved chat with a named adult moderator. Ask each volunteer to pick a crew, then choose Mission 01 or 02 with the director.

Hey! Welcome to H4 at [school]. We’re working together to bring dance, media and leadership opportunities to our school. Pick Dance, Media or Ops. I’ll help you choose one small task this week. If you get stuck, just ask!
Give everyone one task

Use your approved lead account in H4Hours. Open the volunteer’s card in the Lead Dashboard. Paste one task, set the due date, save, and check that it appears. Share the task card through the approved channel.

Hey team! Our goal this week is [goal]. [Name], your task is [task]. Please check H4Hours and let me know by Tuesday if it works for you. Aim to finish by Friday. If you need help or a different task, tell me.
Find a task to assign
Run your school group chat

Keep it about team work. Offer an alternative to students who can’t join the chat. Use approved channels, keep personal details out, and give everyone space after hours.

Read the chat rules.

Check in during the week
  • Monday: Choose one goal and assign tasks.
  • Tuesday: Check that everyone understands their job.
  • Wednesday–Thursday: Help anyone who is stuck.
  • Friday: Check proof, thank your team and send an update.
Great work this week! We finished [team result]. Thank you for helping. Next week we’re working on [goal]. If you have an idea, let us know!
Review completed work

Check the proof in H4Hours if your access allows it. Ask for one specific detail if something is unclear. Adults handle official hours approval.

Help someone who is stuck

Send one friendly check-in in an approved channel. Offer a smaller task or a break. If the student still doesn’t reply, ask the director for help in private.

Hey [name], how is your task going? No worries if you got busy. Do you need help, more time or a different task?
Run your first team meeting

Ask the director or a designated adult to help run the meeting.

  1. 0–5 min: Meet the team.
  2. 5–10 min: Explain the three crews and let people choose.
  3. 10–15 min: Pick a mission and explain the goal.
  4. 15–25 min: Assign one task each and set due dates.
  5. 25–30 min: Explain how to send proof and reach an adult.
Send a Friday update

Share a short update through your approved channel. Keep personal information and student records out.

School: ___ Mission: ___ Tasks assigned: ___ | Done: ___ | Stuck: ___ One win: ___ One thing we need help with: ___ Next week’s goal: ___
Know when to ask an adult

Adults handle approvals, money, permission forms, complaints and official hours. If H4Hours fails, tell the director what screen and error you saw. Ask the director what to do next. Record the task when H4Hours works again.

Hi, our school is [school]. We are working on [mission]. We are stuck because [reason]. Can you help us with [specific question]?
